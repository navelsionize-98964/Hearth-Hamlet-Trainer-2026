It combines resource management, idle progression, kingdom building, research, policy decisions, trade, magic, and light strategic defense in a cozy pixel-art setting.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Hearth and Hamlet**. This trainer enhances the kingdom-management loop by modifying resource production, construction, research, worker management, policies, trade, magic, and defensive systems. It is designed for single-player use and helps players experiment with settlement layouts, upgrade paths, and economic strategies without removing the game's core city-building mechanics.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to test different kingdom layouts, explore research and magic paths, accelerate idle progression, experiment with policies and trade, or enjoy a relaxed city-building experience with fewer resource constraints.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Hearth and Hamlet is a top-down city-builder rather than a character-focused action game. Use the normal map controls to navigate between settlement areas and points of interest. The trainer does not alter map navigation; its movement-related benefit is faster access to the systems and locations needed for building, trade, research, and defense.

### Tools & Inventory

The game focuses on settlement stockpiles rather than a traditional character inventory. Monitor food, wood, stone, gold, and other available resources through the kingdom interface. Activate the resource option before spending materials on buildings, research, policies, trade, or magical development.

### World & Environment

Begin with a campfire and expand through a medieval pixel-art world containing forests, lakes, rivers, magical swamps, neighboring kingdoms, and distant trading lands. Assign workers, construct facilities, research technologies, establish trade routes, and use policies to shape the growth of the settlement.

### Combat & Weapons

Defense is handled through recruited soldiers, city guard decisions, defensive fortifications, and kingdom management rather than direct real-time character combat. Use the trainer's defensive options before major threats or invasions, then keep sufficient workers and resources available to sustain the settlement after the danger passes.
